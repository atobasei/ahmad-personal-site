Tree of the day

American Beech

Fagus grandifolia

Easy to recognize even from a distance — smooth, pale gray bark that stays smooth for the tree's whole life, unlike almost every other hardwood. Beeches hold their papery leaves through much of winter, so a beech grove in January still looks half-dressed.

Range · Eastern U.S. and SE Canada
