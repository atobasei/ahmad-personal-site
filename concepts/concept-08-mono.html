Tree of the day

American Beech

Fagus grandifolia

Easy to recognize even from a distance — smooth, pale gray bark that stays smooth for the tree's whole life, unlike almost every other hardwood. Beeches tend to hold onto their dried, papery leaves through much of winter, so a beech grove in January still looks half-dressed.

That smooth bark is also why beech trees are the most carved-on trees in America — old initials can stay legible for decades.

Range
Eastern U.S. and southeastern Canada
